      Phrases the line

      THE ASSIGNMENT
      
          
      The customer notes are about the first screen. The solution is that screen.
      
          
      Nova runs Harbor’s first recruiter screen. The assignment is twelve customer notes: questions stacked before someone finished, vague answers counted as done, a named project was skipped, the AI line was missed, a fair question ended the call, the close was too fast, and the recruiter saw a score with no reason. Two notes are results, not causes. Engineer screens convert worse, and two later hires came back Medium.
      
          
      Where to look. Tickets are the notes. Priorities say what is in this build. This page says how each note shows up in the interview. Solution is the interview, in chat or voice, with an interviewee view and a recruiter view of the same call.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      
            
      WHAT IS BUILT
      One call, two ways to sit in it
      
            Chat and voice share the controller
          
      
          
      
            
      INTERVIEWEE
      Takes the screen
      They talk with Nova in chat or voice. The opening names Nova as an AI recruiter, names the role, says they can stop, and asks the first question. A timer starts when their first message appears. Ending the interview tells them it has ended and they can leave.
      
            
      RECRUITER
      Reads the same call
      They see the live transcript, the call journey, the role goals, and the interview summary. Sample replies sit under the transcript so a vague answer, a named project, pay, an unfinished sentence, or an off-topic line can be tried. The interviewee does not see those samples or the summary.

      HOW A TURN IS CHOSEN
      The last answer picks the next line
      
          
      
          
      
            
      NV-2 · VAGUE WORK
      Stay on the same goal
      “I do backend and stuff” does not finish the goal. Nova uses a few of their words and asks once for one real case. A second general answer moves on.
      
            
      NV-2 · “I DON’T KNOW”
      Ask if the question is unclear
      If they say they don’t know, Nova asks whether the question is confusing or just hard to answer. It does not recite the goal again. If they are confused, the aim is said in plainer words.
      
            
      NV-9 · A NAMED DETAIL
      Follow that detail once
      A tool, project, number, or specific moment gets one question about that thing. If they already gave a reason and a result, Nova accepts it and moves to the next goal.
      
            
      NV-4 · UNFINISHED
      Pause
      A trailing “and…” or a clear pause gets a short backchannel. Nova asks nothing new and does not spend the pause on the next goal.
      
            
      NV-8 · THEIR QUESTION
      Answer from the brief
      Team, place, and hours come from that role’s knowledge. Pay is not there, so Nova says it will not guess, asks if they want to keep going, and waits.
      
            
      OFF THE GOAL
      Come back, then move
      A hello is greeted. Small talk is acknowledged and brought back to the question already asked. A second time off the goal, Nova leaves it and moves on.

      HOW THE SCREEN IS BUILT
      The controller decides. The model speaks.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      01
      Interview controller
      It owns the opening, the three goals, pauses, the close, and when the screen is done. Chat and voice both send the candidate’s words here. The recruiter journey is this same state.
      
            
      02
      Role briefs and retrieval
      Engineer, restaurant, and tutor each have their own goals, tone, and brief. When the candidate asks something, Nova searches that brief. A fact that is missing stays missing. Pay is missing on purpose.
      
            
      03
      The prompt and the model
      The Prompt page is the instruction for the selected role. Settings holds the model key. The model phrases the line from that instruction and the controller’s decision. It does not skip a goal or invent a job detail.
      
            
      04
      The two views
      The interviewee gets the conversation, the timer, and a clear end. The recruiter gets the transcript, the goals, and the summary after the call. The timer length is set in Settings and starts on the first candidate message.

      WHY THE SCREEN IS SHAPED THIS WAY
      Choices that follow from the notes
      
          
      
            
      Chat stands in for the phone
      The notes are about calls. Chat makes each turn visible while the screen is reviewed. Voice uses the same controller, so speech follows the same rules.
      
            
      Two seats, one transcript
      The interviewee needs the conversation and a clear end. The recruiter needs the journey and the summary the notes asked for. Both sit on the same call.
      
            
      The goals stay
      The notes describe listening failures. The engineer questions stay in order. What changes is how Nova hears an answer and what it asks next.
      
            
      The model phrases, the controller decides
      A hello, an unsure reply, and a vague answer should sound like a conversation. The prompt gives the model the role, the goal, and the rules. It cannot skip a stage or make up pay.
