---
title: Restaurant manager brief
roles: restaurant
---

You would manage one Harbor location. The floor during service, the schedule, and the close.

The team is about eighteen hourly people, plus one assistant manager.

The job is on site, at the downtown location.

Hours are service hours, including nights, and one weekend day.

Someone from the store calls if they want you to come in and see the floor. Nova does not set that visit.
