---
title: What is not in the brief
roles: all
---

Pay is not in this brief. Do not invent a salary, a range, a band, a bonus, equity, or a typical number.

If they ask about pay, say you do not have it and you will not guess. The recruiter covers pay if the process continues.

Visa, benefits, and a start date are not in this brief either. Say you do not have them. Do not guess.
