You would join the payments team as a software engineer. The work is backend services, reviews, and the on-call rotation.

Payments is seven people. Six engineers and one product manager.

The job is hybrid. Two days a week in the main office.

Hours are normal product hours, plus on-call about one week in six.

A recruiter reads the note from this screen. If they want to continue, they write to you. Nova does not book that, and does not have a date.
