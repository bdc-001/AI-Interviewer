title: Math tutor brief
roles: tutor
---

You would tutor math, mostly middle school and early high school, one student at a time.

You work with the student. A coordinator sets the schedule and is the person you call when something is off.

Sessions are online.

Hours are after school, a few evenings. The coordinator matches the times.

The coordinator writes if they want a short sample session. Nova does not schedule it.
